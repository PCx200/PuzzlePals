# PuzzlePals
Puzzle Pals is a game made to be displayed in the central library of Enschede and is made in collaboration with children from the International School Twente. They all made monster drawings and came up with various super powers for their monsters.

Puzzle Pals delivers an opportunity to explore the imagination of children and casually challenges the problem-solving skills of curious minds that are open to a short, positive, and brightening experience while exploring the library.
## Gameplay
In Puzzle Pals, you play as four different monsters in a location called the Puzzle Planes looking for the mythical force that unites all monsters, in a world called The Place. Each monster comes from their own area in the world, ranging from the Candy coast to the Puppet Plaza and the Glass Grotto.

To get to the Mythical force you must prove you're worthy by applying your smarts to get past various obstacles and jump from puzzle to puzzle and from platform to platform.

Each Monster has their own mechanics that can be applied in puzzles to get to the Mythical portal in the end!
